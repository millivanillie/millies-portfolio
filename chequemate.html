The challenge

The brief

For Sheridan Datathon 2026, our team chose Tangerine's "Resilience Rewired" track. The challenge: help customers understand and build their financial resilience using real banking data.

Problem

An unexpected expense, like a job loss, a car repair or a rent increase, can throw anyone's finances off track. Most banking apps only show what has already happened, not how prepared you are for what might happen next.

Solution

ChequeMate shows a customer's finances, applies a financial shock, and builds a realistic plan to recover, with challenges and badges that keep people motivated along the way. After reviewing the data, we identified key patterns and insights that illuminated tools for helping users build their financial resilience.

Discover

We started with the data: what does financial resilience actually look like in real accounts?

The data

We built ChequeMate on historical banking data from 4,500 accounts. Tangerine's dataset includes 9 different .csv files containing clients account numbers, location, income data, and transaction histories.

Insights

  • Insight 1: Users can find it helpful to plan for future financial shocks by calculating different scenarios that take into account their unique financial situations.
  • Insight 2: what made some accounts more resilient than others
  • Insight 3: what this meant for the people behind the accounts
