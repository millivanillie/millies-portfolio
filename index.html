How I design

Nursing → Interaction Design

Working closely with people in vulnerable situations gave me a unique perspective into how people think, work and make decisions.

In the healthcare world, decision making needs to be quick and efficient. Unfortunately, this comes with a lot of stress and it's easy for people to feel lost in the amount of information thrown at them. Once I started noticing this in my job, I started seeing it in other places too – people attempting to navigate through systems that felt like they had been designed with little to no understanding of what they actually needed.

Maintaining a willingness to learn and be curious is key when it comes to understanding the world around us. When I design, my goal is for people to feel this human connection beyond the screen. It goes beyond stylish aesthetics or what's popular. It's about creating spaces that demonstrate empathy, understanding and trust, and is grounded in real user experiences.
